Appendix K collects full proofs of results stated in the main text, in the terse style of the chapters but with every step. It proves Sanov's theorem for a finite alphabet by the method of types (upper bound by summing type class probabilities, lower bound by a sequence of types converging to an interior point), shows that the I projection onto a moment constraint set is an exponential tilt and derives the Pythagorean identity for relative entropy, proves Ville's inequality for nonnegative supermartingales by optional stopping, derives Tweedie's formula for the posterior mean under a normal likelihood, and sketches the risk optimal threshold for testing a point null against a unit information prior, which gives a critical score of order the square root of log n. The tools are types and type classes, Lagrange multipliers, stopped supermartingales, Gaussian integrals and the Laplace expansion. This is background and proof material: the appendix proves results stated in earlier chapters and adds no new theorem of its own. The threshold argument is labeled a sketch and is not a full proof.
