This appendix is background. It collects the martingale theory behind the predictive and testing chapters: martingales, supermartingales and submartingales adapted to a filtration, stopping times and the optional stopping theorem under boundedness or uniform integrability, and Doob's martingale convergence theorem for L1 bounded martingales and nonnegative supermartingales. It states Doob's maximal inequality for nonnegative submartingales and Ville's inequality, its anytime valid form for a nonnegative supermartingale with initial mean at most one, which gives probability at most one over c that the process ever reaches c. It also states that any nonnegative process with expectation at most one at every stopping time is dominated by a supermartingale of initial mean at most one, so e processes are, up to domination, the maximal class of anytime valid evidence, and it recalls the martingale central limit theorem used for Gaussian limits. The posterior mean martingale and the martingale posterior converge by Doob's theorem.

## From the literature

Optional stopping and Doob's martingale convergence theorem. Ville's inequality (Ville 1939). Doob's maximal inequality. The martingale central limit theorem. Anytime valid inference through nonnegative supermartingales and e processes (Shafer and Vovk 2019), and the domination of e processes by nonnegative supermartingales (Ramdas, Ruf, Larsson and Koolen 2022).
