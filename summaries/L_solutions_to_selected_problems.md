Appendix L gives solutions to representative problems from the chapters. Short solutions come first, grouped under 41 topic headings, among them the predictive paradigm, the method of types, Sanov and I projection, Bayes risk and testing, MDL, the horseshoe prior, e values and stopping, variational inference, nonparametric predictive risk, maximum entropy algorithms, deviation regimes, Bayesian asymptotics, data augmentation, deep learning, Gaussian processes, Bayesian trees, hierarchical models, Dirichlet processes, model criticism, state space filtering, Hamiltonian and Langevin Monte Carlo, particle MCMC, portfolio choice, Bayesian optimization, Thompson sampling, reinforcement learning, likelihood free inference, double machine learning, prediction powered inference and generative Bayesian computation. Eighteen fully worked solutions follow, among them the martingale property of the predictive, the Sanov rate for a linear constraint, the log Bayes factor as a random walk, Tweedie's formula, the horseshoe shrinkage weight, Bernstein and von Mises as curvature, maximum entropy under moment constraints, the Kelly fraction, the rate distortion Lagrangian, an e value from a mixture likelihood ratio, Polya Gamma augmentation, the Gaussian process predictive, the ELBO decomposition, the Kalman update, particle filter weights, Thompson sampling as posterior probability matching, Neyman orthogonality, and generative Bayesian computation as transport. This is a collection of worked solutions, background rather than new theory.
