Appendix S collects the lemmas behind the componentwise master inequality of the tilted de Finetti synthesis and its tuning corollary. The setting is exchangeable prediction under a moment constraint: a strictly positive reference law P on a finite alphabet, a convex window of empirical distributions around the constraint set, its I projection, and the law of a fixed block of coordinates given that the empirical distribution lies in the window. Three lemmas are proved under the standing assumptions of that section: window stability (the I projection moves by a constant times the window width), the existence of an inner window type with divergence within order 1 over N of the minimum, which gives a lower bound on the window probability, and a concentration bound on types far from the I projection that follows from the method of types and the divergence gap (a Pinsker type inequality). Combined with the Heath and Sudderth mixture representation they prove the master inequality and the tuning corollary, which takes the split radius delta_N equal to the square root of (k+1) log(N+1) over N, so that the mass of far types is at most a constant over (N+1) squared. The lemmas are technical and serve one proof, so none is catalogued separately.
