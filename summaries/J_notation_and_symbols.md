This appendix is background and collects notation, not results. It repeats the main entries of the front matter Notation section, which is the full list, including the main local reuses of its letters. It fixes conventions: random variables capital, realizations lower case, equality to exponential order in n, empirical distribution or type, Kullback Leibler divergence in nats, Shannon entropy, mutual information, scoring rules and log loss, the directing measure and prior law of an exchangeable sequence, loss, utility, risk and Bayes risk, the log partition function and Legendre conjugate, large deviation rate functions, Fisher information, exponential tilts and I projections (with the reverse I projection), Bayes factors, e variables and e processes, convergence modes, the moderate deviation scale, global and local shrinkage scales, the horseshoe shrinkage weight, the proximal operator, the evidence lower bound, the Polya Gamma and half Cauchy distributions, and the risk optimal Bayesian testing threshold of order root log n.

## From the literature

Standard notation of information theory (Cover and Thomas 2006) and Bayesian statistics. No theorems are presented.
