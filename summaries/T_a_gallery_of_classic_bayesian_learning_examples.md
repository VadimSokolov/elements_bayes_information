# Appendix T. A Gallery of Classic Bayesian Learning Examples

Appendix T is a gallery of classic Bayesian learning problems worked in the predictive spirit of the book: a prior is a bundle of imagined observations, the posterior is the arithmetic of adding real ones, and the predictive is what remains when the parameter is integrated out. It covers conjugate updating (the coin and Laplace's rule of succession, rate, normal mean, mean and variance, category probabilities, overdispersion), named puzzles (the German tank and taxicab problems, naive Bayes, A/B testing, James and Stein shrinkage with the batting averages data, the Krichevsky and Trofimov predictor), sequential prediction and coding, concentration and PAC Bayes bounds, decision problems (the newsvendor, Wald's sequential test, the secretary problem, Gittins indices), the Kalman filter and hidden Markov models, Gaussian process regression and classification, model averaging, Good and Turing, the Bayesian bootstrap, the Laplace approximation, Elo and TrueSkill, calibration, conformal prediction, Jeffreys prior and Stein's unbiased risk estimate. Each example is a small illustration of a standard method and the appendix proves no new theorem.

## From the literature
